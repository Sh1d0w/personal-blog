Hi, I'm **Radoslav** (aka Sh1d0w). I'm a senior fullstack developer and AI researcher with more than 15 years of experience building software and pushing the boundaries of artificial intelligence.

- **Self-hosting** — Platforms, tools, and infrastructure for running services at home
- **Fullstack development** — Architecture, performance, and best practices
- **AI research** — Insights from the field and hands-on techniques
